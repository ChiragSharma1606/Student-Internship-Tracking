How InternTrack Works

From internship assignment to final certification — managed seamlessly.

1
Get Assigned

Students are assigned to industry partners by faculty. All internship details are set up in the system.

2
Submit Weekly Reports

Every week, students log their progress, learnings, and tasks. Faculty reviews and approves each submission.

3
Get Certified

On successful completion, certificates are issued and tracked in the system for future reference.
